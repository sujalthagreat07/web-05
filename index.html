CYBERSECURITY AWARENESS

Not Everything Hidden Is Illegal

The deep web is much larger than the dark web. Private email accounts, university systems, cloud storage, and online banking are examples of deep-web content.

The dark web can provide privacy and anonymity, but it can also contain scams, malicious software, stolen information, and other security threats.

STAY SAFE ONLINE

Basic Cybersecurity Practices

🔐

Use Strong Passwords

Create unique passwords and avoid reusing the same password across different accounts.

🛡️

Enable 2FA

Two-factor authentication adds another layer of protection to your online accounts.

⚠️

Avoid Suspicious Links

Be careful with unexpected links, downloads, and requests for sensitive information.
